Our mission
To make the drink that's good for you the one you actually want.
Why we brew

All About Kombucha started in a Galway kitchen with a simple frustration: the healthy option in the fridge was never the one anyone wanted to drink. We set out to brew something that tasted as good as it made you feel.

We believe what you drink matters as much as what you eat. Every bottle is naturally fermented, full of live cultures and made with real, organic ingredients. No sweeteners, no shortcuts, nothing we wouldn't give our own families.

We brew in Galway, in a carbon-neutral brewery, and we buy from growers we know by name, like Charles. Doing it properly matters more to us than doing it fast.

Brewed properly
Slow, natural fermentation with live cultures. We never pasteurise or cut corners.
Rooted in Galway
Local growers, a local team and a brewery that stays part of its community.
Kind to the planet
A carbon-neutral brewery, recyclable packaging and less waste in every batch.
“We wanted to make something our friends would reach for over a can of cola. Every new brew still has to pass that test.”
Keith, All About Kombucha

Home brewing guide · 14 min watch
How to brew your own kombucha at home
You'll need
1 SCOBY + 200ml starter liquid
2L filtered water
6 bags black or green tea
150g white sugar
A large glass jar, cloth and a band
Flip-top bottles for second ferment
Timing
Prep30 min
First ferment7–10 days
Second ferment2–3 days
Step by step
▶ 1:40
Step 1
Brew the sweet tea
Steep tea in 500ml hot water, stir in the sugar, then top up with cold water.
▶ 2:05
Step 2
Add the SCOBY
Once it's at room temperature, pour into the jar and add the SCOBY and starter.
▶ 1:15
Step 3
First ferment
Cover with cloth and leave somewhere warm and dark. Taste from day 7.
▶ 2:30
Step 4
Flavour and bottle
Bottle with fruit or ginger, seal, and leave 2–3 days for the fizz. Then chill.
