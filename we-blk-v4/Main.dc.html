Why each section is there
  1. 1

    Announcement bar

    States both savings, the 40% sale and the extra £64, on every page. The asterisk links to the size condition in the footer.

  2. 2

    Short hero, site styling

    Uses the live site’s torn edge and hand-drawn underline so it feels part of Warren Evans. Kept compact so the three options sit near the top.

  3. 3

    Three distinct tiles

    White cards on the beige background used on the live bed pages, so shoppers read them as three separate options to compare.

  4. 4

    Set roundel

    A small round badge numbers each set, which makes them easy to refer to in ads, emails and on the phone.

  5. 5

    Bed + mattress strip

    The bed and mattress sit together on a beige strip under the hero, each with its own price, so the card reads as one unit and it is clear both items are included.

  6. 6

    The three mattresses

    Each option has a different mattress, so this explains the difference in one glance, with Warren’s own words for founder trust.

  7. 7

    How your order works

    Answers delivery, assembly, recycling and trial questions before they turn into reasons not to buy.

  8. 8

    Existing USPs

    The site’s current four USPs, kept as they are so the page stays familiar.

  9. 9

    Reviews and trust

    Real reviews from the current site that mention mattress comfort and old-bed removal, backed by the Trustpilot count and awards.

  10. 10

    Showrooms and video calls

    A big purchase many people want to try first. Gives shoppers not ready to buy online a clear next step.
