6d
Our concept: your numbers, both years
Competitors show a fixed calculation for one year. This version adds two things they don't have:

1. A usage slider. Most people guess their usage, so they can see how the saving changes if the guess is wrong.

2. What happens after month 12. The discount ends and this tariff can become dearer than the current one. Power to Switch offers a reminder, so the calculation leads to a reason to come back.

Drag the slider to try it. The reminder is a proposed feature. Current rate is assumed to be 39.98p/kWh.
