01
Where it was made
Knitted, linked and finished in our own factory in Baldoyle, Dublin.
Knitwear House, Baldoyle Industrial Estate, Dublin 13, D13 DT63. Designed in-house and made on site — not sourced, not subcontracted. You can collect this piece at our reception desk if you'd rather come to it than have it come to you.
02
Fibre
95% wool, 5% cashmere. 100% natural.
Spun and dyed by accredited mills. Our bespoke colours are designed in-house and dyed exclusively for IrelandsEye — Blue Ocean is a light denim blue with contrasting flecks.
OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Responsible Wool Standard The Good Cashmere Standard
03
Construction
Chunky jersey knit, hand-linked shoulders and neck.
A plain jersey stitch at a heavier gauge, so the sweater holds its shape rather than draping. Panels are joined on the linking machine stitch by stitch — a slower join than overlocking, and the reason the seams sit flat under the arm.
04
How it feels, fits and wears
Relaxed fit. Soft against the neck, warm without weight.
Fit Relaxed through body and sleeve
On the model 188cm / 6'2, wearing size L
Size equivalent UK/US 40–42 · IT/FR/EU 50
Wear Softens with wear; may pill early on
05
Who made it
27 pairs of hands, from yarn cone to folded box.
Knitters, linkers, menders, pressers and the quality table. Every piece is inspected by hand before it leaves the building.
Linking, Baldoyle
Final inspection
06
How to care for it
Hand wash warm, dry flat, store folded.
Hand wash in lukewarm water. Do not bleach. Do not tumble dry. Dry flat and reshape while damp. Warm iron. Store folded in a cool, dry place — never on a hanger.
