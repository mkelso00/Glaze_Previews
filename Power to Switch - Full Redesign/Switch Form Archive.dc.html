8e
Meter number help
The field most likely to stop a switch. A bill sketch shows where the MPRN sits, the field checks the number as it's typed, and a common mistake (typing the account number) gets its own message. Customers who can't find it can ask for it to be looked up from their address. Type in the box, or try the sample buttons. The bill layout needs checking against real NI bills.
