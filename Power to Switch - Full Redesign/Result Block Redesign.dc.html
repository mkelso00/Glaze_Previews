1Individual result block: three directions
Current PTS block
  • Tariff name is light grey, the weakest text in the card.
  • No saving shown. The user has to do the maths against their current bill.
  • The right column, where the eye lands for price and action, holds a bill in body-size text and "Not available through 'Power to Switch'".
  • No button, so no obvious next step.
  • Pay As You Go, 12 Month Term, Variable Rate and New Customers are styled as buttons but aren't clickable.
  • No monthly cost, standing charge or exit fee.
Worth taking from competitors
  • bonkers.ie: the saving is the biggest number, with one strong CTA directly under it.
  • bonkers.ie and Switcher: a fixed fact grid (term, payment, billing, rate type) with label over value, so cards scan in columns.
  • Switcher: the exit fee sits in the main grid, not in the T&Cs.
  • bonkers.ie: rates are behind a "Show rates" toggle, which keeps the card short.
  • Their gap: none show a monthly figure or say what happens when you press the button.
Principles
  • Price, saving and action are grouped on the right and show once each.
  • The saving is compared against the user's current tariff and includes cashback, with a label saying so.
  • Fixed fact slots that always appear, showing "None" when a fee is zero.
  • Perks read as ticks. Only tags that change the price or eligibility stay as chips.
  • The CTA names the destination, and a line under it explains the route.
Sample: SSE Airtricity 1 Year Keypad Electricity 10.5% + £30 Welcome Credit vs a current bill of £1,279.45. The Tweaks panel switches the CTA route, cashback handling and a "costs more" result.
