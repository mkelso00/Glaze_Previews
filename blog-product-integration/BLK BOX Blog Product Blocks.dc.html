Home Gym Rack Attachments Buying Guide

Katie McGonigle · July 9, 2026

Your rack should do more than hold a barbell. With the right attachments, it becomes the centrepiece of your home gym, adapting to your training as your goals evolve.

Make Your Rack Work Harder

A modular rack gives you the flexibility to train the way you want without filling your gym with standalone machines. Adding attachments lets you increase exercise variety, save floor space and build a complete strength setup over time.

Unlock More Exercises

Dip Station

A staple in any strength programme. The Dip Station adds one of the most effective bodyweight movements to your rack, helping develop pressing strength and upper body muscle. Simple, durable and highly effective.

Leg Extension & Leg Curl Attachment

Complete your lower body training without investing in another machine. Isolate the quadriceps and hamstrings, helping build balanced leg strength while supporting your compound lifts.

Hip Thrust Attachment

Strong glutes support stronger lifts. A stable, comfortable setup for one of the most effective lower body exercises — removing the hassle of balancing benches and barbells.

Every athlete trains differently, which is why your rack should be built around your goals. Add storage to stay organised. Add attachments to unlock new movements.
