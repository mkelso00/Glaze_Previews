Liberation Oil: presenting the subscription

Five concepts for the product page, shown on mobile where most Meta traffic lands. Each numbered dot has a note beside it. Tap a dot or a note to highlight the pair.

Check before we build

  1. How long does a bottle really last? At 2 to 3 drops a day, 40ml should last far longer than 8 weeks. If so, deliveries pile up and people cancel. Reorder data will tell us.
  2. 'Free gift every 2nd month' and '2 month commitment' don't match 8 and 10 week deliveries. What are the real terms?
  3. Subscription orders are £18.75, under the £25 free delivery line. Do subscribers pay postage?
  4. What is the gift? A candle was mentioned.
  5. The crossed-out £0.00 on the live page looks like a theme bug.

Quick wins in Loop, no build

  • Rename the plan heading to 'Delivered every'
  • Rename plans to '8 weeks (daily use)' and '10 weeks (most days)'
  • Turn on the benefits pop-up
  • Show the per-delivery price
  • Show frequency as buttons, not a dropdown
