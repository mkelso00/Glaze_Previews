How people get to the quiz
ASocial ads
Meta and TikTok ads ask "Which van do you drive?". Each van links straight into the quiz with it already picked.
Bsignsdiy.co.uk
US visitors on the UK site see a "Coming to the US, get 15% off" bar that opens the quiz.
CEmail and followers
The UK list and social followers get the link to pass on. Sharing earns an extra 5%.
DLanding page
The hero link and the "Not sure what you need?" block send unsure visitors to the quiz.
