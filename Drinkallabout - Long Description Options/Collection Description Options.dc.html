Ár gCumbaitea

Organic kombucha, brewed in Connemara

Live cultures, real fruit, no concentrate. Every bottle is fermented slowly in our carbon-neutral brewery in Co. Galway.

Kombucha is fermented green and black tea, brewed with a live culture until the sugar is eaten away and what is left is lightly sparkling, gently sour and full of living bacteria. Ours takes fourteen days. Nothing is pasteurised, nothing is made from concentrate, and every flavour in this collection is certified organic.

How to choose your flavour

  • New to kombucha? Start with Raspberry — the sweetest and least vinegary of the range.
  • Swapping out a fizzy drink? Ginger + Lemon has the sharpest bite.
  • Drinking it with food? Blueberry + Mint sits well alongside anything spiced.

Delivery, storage and shelf life

Cases of twelve ship nationwide in Ireland, chilled and insulated, usually within two working days. Keep it refrigerated. Unopened bottles last three months from delivery; once opened, drink within five days. Subscriptions can be paused, skipped or cancelled at any point from your account.
